## Description (1977/4000)
```
Plekio helps you stay on track with your treatment. Plan your medication courses, get a reminder when a dose is due, and log it in one tap: in the app, from the lock screen, on a widget or with Siri.

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no analytics.

TODAY AT A GLANCE
• Your doses for the day, grouped into morning, afternoon and evening
• The "Up next" card logs a whole time slot at once
• Every log can be undone
• Once the day is done, see a summary and your streak

TREATMENT COURSES
• Dosage, times of day, every-N-days schedules, start and end dates
• Before, with or after meals, or on an empty stomach
• Track your stock and get a reminder before you run out
• Finished courses move to History; repeat one with new dates in seconds
• Travelling? An 08:00 dose stays at 08:00 in a new time zone

REMINDERS THAT WORK
• Take, Snooze or Skip straight from the notification
• The app icon badge shows the doses still due
• Optional reminders for the diary, blood pressure and low stock

WELL-BEING DIARY
• Mood, energy, discomfort, sleep, water, symptoms and milestones
• Progress photos with side-by-side comparison
• Blood pressure and pulse readings with a chart

REPORT FOR YOUR DOCTOR
Create a PDF of your doses, well-being and blood pressure for any period, and share it before your appointment.

WIDGETS AND SIRI
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: Up next, Day progress, Today, Low stock, Diary and Blood pressure
• Log a dose or today's mood right from the widget
• Just say "Log my medications in Plekio", "What's my next medication in Plekio" or "Log my blood pressure in Plekio"

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data is stored only on your device
• Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode
• Widgets hide the details while your iPhone is locked

Works on iPhone and iPad, in light and dark mode, with Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.

Plekio is a tracking tool and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions.
```
