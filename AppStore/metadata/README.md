## App Review notes (English)
```
No account or sign-in is needed. All data is stored locally on the device; the app has no server and collects no data.

Plekio is a medication tracker. It records the doses, schedules and diary entries the user enters and reminds them at the times they set. It does not give medical advice, diagnose or recommend treatment (Guideline 1.4.1); a disclaimer is in the onboarding and in the Terms of Use.

To try it: complete the short onboarding, add a course on the Courses tab (any medication name, two times of day), then log or skip doses on Today, from a notification, a widget or Siri ("Log my medications in Plekio").

The app declares NSSupportsLiveActivities only so the widget's buttons can run their App Intents in the app process; it never starts a Live Activity.
```
