# Plekio Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Plekio is a medication tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app. In short: **it stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, see it, or share it.**

## What the app stores

Everything you enter is stored only on your device:

- treatment courses, medications, dose times and the doses you log or skip;
- diary entries: mood, energy, sleep, water, symptoms, notes and milestones;
- blood pressure and pulse readings;
- photos you attach to medications or diary entries, and your profile picture;
- your profile: name, date of birth, allergies and conditions, if you fill them in;
- your settings, such as theme, reminders and app lock.

There is no account and no server. The app does not send this information anywhere.

## Widgets

If you add Plekio's widgets, they show today's doses, medication names, your progress, low stock, today's mood and your latest blood pressure readings on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. The app keeps a small summary for them on your device, next to its other data. While your iPhone is locked with a passcode, iOS shows the widgets as placeholders, so nothing from Plekio is visible on the Lock Screen until you unlock. Tapping a check or a mood on a widget logs it in the app, as the reminder's Take button does.

## Siri and Shortcuts

You can ask Siri to log your doses, tell you what to take next, or save a blood pressure reading. Plekio carries out the request on your iPhone and stores the result like anything you enter in the app. What you say to Siri, including the numbers of a reading, is processed by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); Plekio receives only the result, never the recording.

Siri works with Plekio only while your iPhone is unlocked. Siri's answers can name your medications and readings, and anyone nearby may hear them. If you turn on Plekio's app lock, Siri leaves the names and numbers out and asks you to open the app instead. You can turn off Siri for Plekio in iOS Settings → Apps → Plekio → Siri.

## What we collect

Nothing. Plekio has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, identifiers or location.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photo library** — only to attach a photo you choose. Photos are saved inside the app and nowhere else.
- **Notifications** — dose, diary, blood pressure and refill reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Face ID / passcode** — the optional app lock uses iOS. The app never receives your biometric data; iOS only tells it whether unlocking succeeded.

## Sharing

Information leaves the app only when you choose to send it:

- **Doctor report (PDF)** — you pick the period and sections and share the file yourself, by any app you choose.
- **Feedback** — "Send feedback" opens your mail app with a message to us. We receive only what you decide to send, and use it only to reply and improve the app.

## Ratings

Now and then the app may ask you to rate it, using Apple's standard dialog. Apple handles the rating; the app doesn't learn whether you rated it or what you chose. You can turn these requests off for all apps in iOS Settings → App Store → In-App Ratings & Reviews.

## Backups

If iCloud Backup or computer backups are turned on, iOS includes the app's data in your device backup, as it does for other apps. Those backups are handled by Apple and protected by your Apple Account.

## Deleting your data

In **Settings → Manage your data** you can delete the diary, the history of finished courses, or all your data at once. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data from the device. Deleted data cannot be restored.

## Children

Plekio is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
