# Plekio Support

Plekio is a medication tracker for iPhone: treatment courses, dose reminders, a health diary and blood pressure, all kept on your device.

## Contact

Write to [plekio.support@gmail.com](mailto:plekio.support@gmail.com), or use **Settings → Send feedback** in the app. We usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**How do I move my data to a new iPhone?**
Plekio's data is part of your regular iPhone backup. Set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup and the app comes back with everything in it.

**Reminders don't arrive.**
Check that notifications are allowed for Plekio in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. Opening the app refreshes the upcoming reminders.

**How do I delete my data?**
In **Settings → Manage your data** you can delete the diary, the history of finished courses, or everything at once. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. Deleted data cannot be restored.

**How do I use Plekio with Siri?**
Open Plekio once after installing it, then say, for example, "Log my medications in Plekio", "What's my next medication in Plekio" or "Log my blood pressure in Plekio". The same actions are in the Shortcuts app, where you can also add them to the Action button or a Home Screen shortcut. Siri works with Plekio only while your iPhone is unlocked. With the app lock on, Siri doesn't say medication names or readings.

**I turned on the app lock and can't get in.**
The lock uses your iPhone's Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. If Face ID fails, the passcode always works.
